## Egypt-first jurisdiction design input (from 01-codes)
- Keep these layers separate:
  - building law and executive regulations;
  - HBRC technical codes (code, part, edition);
  - local administrative authority conditions;
  - civil defence;
  - utilities;
  - environment;
  - professional registration and classification.
- **No Egyptian code edition is verified in any source.** Each project must record the official document, its number, part, edition and enacting instrument. The pack must never infer one from the HBRC portal page.
- International standards (ACI, ASCE, NFPA, ISO, …) apply only by national adoption or contractual reference.
- The Saudi occupancy-certificate / electricity-release rule must not be imported into the Egypt pack.

## "Activation inputs that must never be invented" (12 classes)
Project identity; competent jurisdiction; legal and code references; contract data; names and authorities; dates; control thresholds; data and system definitions; AI and privacy details; vendor and worker data; environmental and social baselines; maturity targets. This matches the Constitution's prohibition list. These become **mandatory onboarding inputs** in the tenant- and project-provisioning design (DFS-004 / GC-31): fields stay empty and blocking until an authorised source fills them.
