# Owner Vision Directive 003 (OVD-003) — recorded 2026-11-26

Source: the owner's own words in the working sessions of 2026-11-23 to 2026-11-26 (Arabic), recorded so nothing is
lost between sessions. It extends OVD-001 and OVD-002; it does not replace them. Nothing here is a claim that anything
exists in the code. Status of each item is tracked in the master map that follows this directive.

## 1. Scale and standard (owner, 2026-11-23 / 2026-11-26)
- The product is a global-level, unique, integrated system - not a local one. It is to compete strongly in the
  Egyptian, Arab and Gulf markets first, and be raised afterwards to European and American level.
- It is to replace a list of 451 programs used to run companies, contracting and engineering in all disciplines,
  covering every department, discipline, task, responsibility and job, for groups the size of Orascom, Emaar or
  Al Shafar, managed digitally and dynamically with current technology.
- It is a product sold to many companies (each company enters its own data; nothing is assumed), managing many
  companies, projects and contracts.
- Required qualities: innovation and originality (not imitation only), world-class patterns, ease and simplicity,
  quality, standards, attractive and distinctive interfaces, and the best programming, databases, platforms and AI
  models available, all at the highest standards and fully tested.
- Every problem has a solution; finding it is the development team's duty. Problems are solved, not handed back to
  the owner. Open-source tools (GitHub and others) may be used; any download of a third-party program or tool is
  reported to the owner first.
- Role given to Claude by the owner: head of the higher committee for programming and development of office,
  administrative and engineering software. No product is released before it has passed every level of review,
  development, innovation, problem solving and gap finding, completely and verifiably.

## 2. Built-in research and reference (owner, 2026-11-23 / 2026-11-26)
- Search inside the program for anything companies need: market information and prices; engineering and
  administrative codes and references (Egyptian, American, Saudi, and the codes of all Arab and Gulf countries);
  and a comprehensive search of approved suppliers. This is part of the program, used by any person looking for
  information.
- Files supplied by the owner come from approved sources with their permission (owner statement 2026-11-23). The
  program records the source and date of every file so each answer, price or code clause traces back to its origin.
- Markets: Egypt, UAE, Saudi Arabia, the Gulf and the Arab countries.

## 3. Construction marketplace (owner, 2026-11-23 - after the research capability)
- An application like Amazon and better, specialised only in building and construction, architecture and decoration,
  with contracts with all approved suppliers in every item. It shares one supplier base with the program
  (approved-supplier directory) and is linked to procurement, stores and cost.

the accountable person approves. No evidence -> no claim; nothing is announced as working before it passes its gate.
